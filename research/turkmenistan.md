# Turkmenistan

**Status:** scouting

**Priority heuristic position:** 23 (after Kyrgyzstan)

## Internet context

Contemporary reports place the first documented full Internet connectivity in Turkmenistan on or about 23 May 1997. An Info-Nets post by Olivier M.J. Crepin-Leblond (23 May 1997) includes a successful traceroute from the UK to 193.232.199.102 (tm.synapse.ru), a sendmail banner confirming the host, and a note that a Russian-language website was live at that IP. The path traversed Russian Space Science Internet (RSSI) infrastructure. This is treated as a verified connectivity milestone (grade B, contemporary network observation).

Dr. Batyr Karryev’s August 1999 report (NSRC Connectivity Provider Database, last updated 25 Oct 1999) states that Academy of Sciences work on Internet access began in 1993 with SIBIS/Sibling&Technologies (SIBNET). Public dial-up access to email and full Internet via SIBNET became available in 1997 over a 9.6 kbit/s leased line to Obninsk, Russia. The same year a 128 kbit/s satellite link to the US supported Turkmenistan Online (online.tm) and SIBNET under the Ministry of Communication. Web sites were prepared in 1997, including sibnet.tm and a Turkmenpress weekly news site (tpress.sibnet.tm). International Turkmen-Turkish University obtained Internet access and a web page in 1998.

Secondary sources consistently date the country’s first Internet access to 1997 via MCI/WorldCom satellite circuits. Public individual access remained extremely limited under Niyazov; private ISPs were later consolidated under Turkmen Telecom.

**.tm ccTLD:** Delegated 30 May 1997 (IANA). Initially managed externally; later associated with NetNames and then TM Domain Registry Ltd (UK). Early second-level registrations were often external. DNS delegation is grade A; it does not by itself prove local web publishing or resident activity.

## Personal web ecology / earliest examples

No verified resident personal homepage or local individual web publishing has been identified in the sources reviewed. Institutional and commercial sites (sibnet.tm, online.tm, government/press pages) appear from 1997 onward. Diaspora or external .tm registrations exist but are not treated as local resident activity without further evidence of connection basis.

## Evidence and chronology

- 1993–: Academy of Sciences / SIBIS planning for Internet access (Karryev 1999, grade C retrospective).
- 23 May 1997: First documented full Internet (traceroute + SMTP banner to tm.synapse.ru) (Crepin-Leblond / NSRC, grade B).
- 30 May 1997: .tm delegated (IANA, grade A for DNS).
- 1997: Public dial-up email/Internet via SIBNET; first local web sites (Karryev 1999, grade B/C).
- 1998: ITU.edu.tm and other institutional pages.
