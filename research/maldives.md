# Maldives — early internet activity

**Status:** scouting (25 September 1996 .mv delegation; 14 October 1996 DhivehiNet 64 kbps satellite launch by Dhiraagu; no verified resident personal homepage yet)

## Internet context

- **.mv ccTLD:** Delegated 25 September 1996; registry Dhivehi Raajjeyge Gulhun PLC (Dhiraagu). Grade A for DNS only (IANA / ICANNWiki / Wikipedia). Primarily used by government and larger entities; open registration with priority for Maldivian connections.
- **First Internet connection / DhivehiNet (14 October 1996):** Dhiraagu introduced dial-up Internet service under the brand DhivehiNet. Contemporary company records state the initial international bandwidth was 64 kbps to Hong Kong Telecom (later second link to Telecom Italia). ITU case study and company press confirm the 14 October 1996 date and 64 kbps satellite link. Grade A/B (operator primary statement + ITU). Service was institutional/commercial; household access remained limited for years (2006 census: ~20% in Malé, ~2.5% in atolls).
- **Earlier telecom:** Dhiraagu established 1 October 1988 as joint venture; national telephone rollout and satellite earth station expansions in the 1990s provided the underlying infrastructure. Packet-switched data services existed before full Internet.
