

## 2026-11-24 — Ariadne 1996 full-text confirmation and personal-page null search

Full text of John Kirriemuir, “Down Your Very Long Way Away,” *Ariadne* issue 2 (1996) confirms and expands the institutional evidence already recorded under FO-04.

Key contemporaneous statements (grade B):
- Research institutions in Tórshavn (including the National Library) connected in January 1995 via 64 kbit/s leased line from the University of the Faroe Islands Department of Natural Sciences to UNI-C in Lyngby, Denmark. Sleipnir server maintained by the university.
- From 29 February 1996 the Faroese Telephone Company (TFL) offered private users Internet access; estimated ~500 users.
- National Library homepage: http://www.sleipnir.fo/flb/flbheim.htm (information about the library + links).
- First Faroese Internet guide: http://www.sleipnir.fo/flb/flbint.htm.
- Additional institutional pages explicitly named: university research page (http://www.sleipnir.fo/research.htm), general Faroe information (http://www.sleipnir.fo/faroe/faroe.htm), library information (http://www.sleipnir.fo/flb/flbst.htm).
- Explicit note that limited-scale users “may even publish a homepage on the net.” This documents the technical possibility of personal/private homepages by early 1996, but names no individual pages or authors.

Source full text (via distantreader mirror of Ariadne): https://distantreader.org/stacks/carrels-patrons/ericleasemorgan/digital-library-journals/txt/journal-ariadne-other--kirriemuir-down_23-1996.txt (accessed 2026-11-24).
