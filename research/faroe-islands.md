## Internet context

The Faroe Islands obtained direct Internet connectivity in January 1995. Research institutions in Tórshavn (including the Faroese National Library) were connected via a 64 kbit/s leased line from the Department of Natural Sciences at the University of the Faroe Islands to UNI-C in Lyngby, Denmark. The university maintained the Sleipnir access server (sleipnir.fo). Dial-up users (mainly libraries, schools, educational institutions) also accessed via Sleipnir.

Private/commercial access via the Faroese Telephone Company (TFL / Telefonverk Føroya) began on 29 February 1996. TFL estimated ~500 Internet users. .fo domain registration date is listed as 14 May 1993 in IANA records.
