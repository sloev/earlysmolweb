**Status:** active scouting; no earliest-personal-page candidate verified.

## Early connectivity and domain context (not personal-page evidence)

### Mid-1990s domain registration
The country-code top-level domain `.mh` appears among domains registered in the mid-1990s in Hobbes’ Internet Timeline compilations (listed with other Pacific and small-state ccTLDs in the 1996–1997 period). Exact IANA delegation date and first live zone details still need primary confirmation.

- Source: Hobbes’ Internet Timeline (RFC 2235 / contemporary compilations listing Marshall Islands (MH) among 1996 domain registrations).
- Evidence grade: **C** (secondary compilation; primary IANA record not yet recovered).

### Late-1990s / early-2000s ISP notes
Secondary sources state that an ISP associated with the “MH” designation was founded around 1996–1997, but also report that the domain was largely inactive for local website registration for a period thereafter. A 2003 RNZ report indicates that the Marshall Islands National Telecommunications Authority (NTA) was still planning to become its own ISP, having previously used ITE in Guam, and was seeking to regain control of the `.mh` domain for local email and websites.

- Sources:
  - Wikipedia “Communications in the Marshall Islands” (archived versions noting ISP “MH” founded 1996–1997 and subsequent inactivity of local website registrations).
  - RNZ, “Marshall Islands plans to set up internet service,” 20 May 2003: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/143667/marshall-islands-plans-to-set-up-internet-service
- Evidence grade: **C/D** (secondary / retrospective; 2003 contemporary for the later NTA plan).

### Infrastructure notes
Telephone and satellite infrastructure existed in the mid-1990s (Majuro, Ebeye, Kwajalein). High-speed undersea-cable connectivity (HANTRU-1) arrived much later (2010). No contemporary 1995–1998 personal-homepage or public web-publishing evidence has been located in readily available sources.

## Research interpretation

- Domain registration and a reported mid-1990s ISP foundation create a plausible window, but the 2003 NTA report suggests local commercial web hosting remained limited or external for some years.
