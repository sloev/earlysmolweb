# Kyrgyzstan — early internet activity

**Status:** scouting  
**ISO:** KG  
**Priority heuristic position:** 22 (after Tajikistan)

## Internet context

Post-Soviet period saw early store-and-forward and UUCP-style networks before full TCP/IP.

- **1991:** “Ala-Too” telecommunications network server in Bishkek, working via Almaty RICC server; ~20 subscribers. Grade B (secondary synthesis of local histories).
- **May 1992:** “Imfiko” firm node obtained official RELCOM status; ~50 subscribers by end of 1992; traffic via Russian networks. Grade B.
- **1994:** Two major providers founded — ElCat (joint state-private, based on the Imfiko/RELCOM node) and AsiaInfo (Kyrgyz branch of Russian Infotel, X.25 then TCP/IP). ElCat provided email and CIS connectivity from 1994; full Internet services package from 1995. AsiaInfo provided X.25 and Internet connectivity. Grade B (company histories and contemporary reports).
- **1995:** Permanent Internet connection established (RFE/RL May 1997 report: Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan 1994, Kyrgyzstan followed in 1995). OSI-RIP provided email starter kits and equipment to schools, university, medical institutions. Silknet (PERDCA/Soros) extended email service including Kyrgyzstan. Grade B.
- Estimates ~500 Internet users and ~5 000 email users by 1997 (RFE/RL).

## Domain

- **.kg** ccTLD delegated 12 July 1995 to AsiaInfo Telecommunication Enterprise (Bishkek). Grade A for DNS delegation (IANA/ICANNWiki). Current registry remains AsiaInfo / cctld.kg. Some secondary sources mention earlier informal registration activity around 1992; primary IANA record is July 1995.

## Personal / community / publishing

No verified resident-authored personal homepage or locally hosted web publication located in current sources. Early activity was institutional/commercial email and ISP nodes (ElCat, AsiaInfo). Public access points and school connections existed by 1995–96. Diaspora or external sites possible but not yet evidenced as Kyrgyz-resident operated.
