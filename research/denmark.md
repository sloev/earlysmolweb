# Denmark — early personal-web research

**Status:** early personal-page candidates identified; earliest claim not independently verified from primary captures.
**Scope:** Denmark as a country entry, separate from Greenland and the Faroe Islands. Pages created by residents in Denmark are distinguished from pages by Danish/Faroese authors who were resident abroad.

## Internet and web-archive context

- The Royal Danish Library's research project *History of the Danish web 1992–1997* studies the early Danish web using material acquired from the Internet Archive and preserved in Netarkivet as WARC files, searchable through SolrWayback. The project places the first Danish website in 1992, major growth in spring 1995, and the opening of .dk domain registration to private individuals in January 1997. This is a key route for systematic extraction of early personal sites.
  - Source: https://www.kb.dk/en/services/cultural-heritage-research-and-study/history-danish-web-1992-1997
  - Project timeline: https://webhistorie.dk/timeline-2/

- Person/project: Familien Madsen Smed.
- Title: family/personal homepage.
- Original URL: not yet established; a virtual museum page preserves a screenshot and secondary discussion.
- Date claimed: November 1995.
- Date type: reported launch/first publication.
- Medium: personal/family homepage.
- Hosting: CompuServe, according to the retrospective account.
- Country connection: Denmark; described as a Danish family publishing a personal homepage. Verify residence and authorship from the original page or corroborating contemporary evidence.
- Status: **candidate; likely important early Danish personal homepage, but not yet independently verified from a contemporary capture.**
- Evidence:
  - A 2019 Danish-language retrospective says the family launched a personal homepage in November 1995, hosted by CompuServe, and reproduces an image credited to Webmuseum.dk. Grade C.
  - A 2016 retrospective says Webmuseum.dk regards the family page as the first private Danish homepage, but does not provide primary evidence in the accessible extract. Grade C.

## Candidate 2: Elin Brimheim Heinesen — first personal homepage, spring 1997

- Person: Elin Brimheim Heinesen.
- Date claimed: spring 1997.
- Medium: personal homepage / online CV and self-presentation.
- Original URL: unknown. Current personal sites are https://heinesen.fo/ and https://heinesen.info/, neither yet tied to the 1997 page.
- Country connection: author residence in Copenhagen, Denmark, at the time. Her biography says she moved back to the Faroe Islands only in 2007 after 25 years in Denmark.
- Status: **candidate; author testimony is direct but retrospective; URL and surviving capture remain unknown.**
- Evidence:
  - In a Q&A published 18 September 2008, Heinesen says she created her first homepage in spring 1997 to learn HTML and as a personal online calling card/CV. She describes it explicitly as personal expression. Grade C (retrospective author testimony).
  - Her biographical page says she returned to Denmark in summer 1993 and lived/worked in Copenhagen for 25 years before moving back to the Faroe Islands in 2007. Grade C for residence chronology.
- Sources:

## Research interpretation

- The 1992 DTU/library site is institutional and must not be counted as personal expression.
- The November 1995 Madsen Smed family page is currently the earliest located Denmark-connected personal-homepage candidate.
- Heinesen's spring 1997 testimony is a valuable named and individually authored personal-page lead, with an explicit Copenhagen residence connection, but the original URL has not yet been found.
- Do not promote either candidate to verified without a primary page/capture or multiple independent contemporary sources.
