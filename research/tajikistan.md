# Tajikistan — early internet activity

**Status:** scouting  
**ISO:** TJ  
**Priority heuristic position:** 21 (after Afghanistan)

## Internet context

Civil-war period (1992–97) produced fragmented telecommunications. First documented internet-related activity was store-and-forward email, not direct TCP/IP.

- **July 1995:** Project for Economic Reform and Development in Central Asia (PERDCA) opened a public electronic-mail site in Dushanbe at the Central Asian Development Agency (CADA). Free scheduled public access for CIS citizens; 14 400-baud dial-in; software from local operator. Contemporary NSRC archive record (August 1995). Grade B. Main server remained in CADA’s Tashkent office; international traffic routed via Tashkent or Moscow. Not direct Internet access inside Tajikistan.
- By 1996 ~700 users (reported estimate). By November 1997 the network linked Leninabad (Khujand public hall), Dushanbe and Kulob; ~1 500 email addresses. CADA donated fax-modems to universities, libraries and charities. Contemporary RFE/RL reports (May and November 1997). Grade B.
- Direct public Internet access: Telecomm Technology chronology dates first public service in Dushanbe to 23 December 1998; Human Rights Watch places full access in January 1999. Grade C retrospective for the exact week; both place it after the email network.

## Domain

- **.tj** ccTLD established May 1997 (IANA / Postel delegation to Irage M. Tursoon-Zadeh, TJ Network Services, Fresno CA, USA; technical contact ProNet / later Karl Watts). Grade A for DNS delegation only; external administration. Redelegated 2003 to Information Technology Center (Dushanbe). First second-level domains registered January 2004.
