# Chile — early internet-based activity

## Scope and attribution
This file separates early email/news networks from TCP/IP Internet access and later Web services. The strongest early evidence presently concerns University of Chile-operated UUCP and BITNET services; it does not identify a personal homepage.

## Category chronology

### 1985 — experimental domestic UUCP network
- Operator: Department of Computer Science, University of Chile, Santiago.
- Service: experimental UUCP network connecting three Unix systems at different universities over slow modems and telephone lines.
- Status: **probable** for the domestic network's establishment in 1985; the account is a retrospective technical history written by participants.
- Source: Ricardo Baeza-Yates, José M. Piquer and Patricio V. Poblete, “The Chilean Internet Connection or I Never Promised You a Rose Garden,” NIC Chile: https://www.nic.cl/estudios/inet93/paper.html
- Caution: a domestic UUCP network is not, by itself, proof of an international Internet connection or public Web service.

### 1986 — international UUCP/email and Usenet access
- The same participant-authored history says the University of Chile tested X.25 connections to INRIA (France) and SEISMO (USA) during 1986, then used long-distance UUCP calls to UUNET.
- The authors state that this first international UUCP connection provided email and Usenet news. Because of cost, a full news feed was received on magnetic tape until 1991.
- Hub host: `uchdcc`, an NCR Tower 1632 at the time.
- Status: **probable**, based on a detailed technical account by the operators, published in 1993; seek contemporary UUCP maps, logs and mail/news headers for verification.
- Source: https://www.nic.cl/estudios/inet93/paper.html

### 1987 — BITNET email network
- The University of Chile Engineering Computer Center established a BITNET link and began connecting computer centres at regional universities. The authors state this enabled email access for scientists across disciplines.
- Status: **probable**, retrospective participant account.
- Source: https://www.nic.cl/estudios/inet93/paper.html

### 1990 — TCP/IP interconnection within the University of Chile
- An Ethernet backbone was installed on the Engineering campus; the Chilean UUCP and BITNET networks were interconnected using TCP/IP.
- Status: **probable**, retrospective participant account. This describes campus internetworking, not yet the country's direct international Internet link.
- Source: https://www.nic.cl/estudios/inet93/paper.html

### January 1992 — direct international Internet links
- The 1993 paper's abstract says Chile had two international 56 Kbps Internet links since January 1992. It explains that REUNA and Unired were separate networks and that both appeared in MERIT routing tables in February 1992.
- Status: **probable/high confidence**, near-contemporary technical paper by network participants. Confirm with contemporaneous routing tables or NSF/MERIT records before calling this fully verified.
- Source: https://www.nic.cl/estudios/inet93/paper.html

### May 1993 — public-facing network services and FTP/email
- The paper documents email and FTP use over REUNA and a national mailing-list dispute about international traffic charges. It describes public FTP servers as a practical concern in pricing policy and reports ten universities connected to REUNA by May 1993.
- Status: **probable** for the service ecology by May 1993; the paper does not identify the earliest specific FTP server or mailing list.
- Source: https://www.nic.cl/estudios/inet93/paper.html

## Evidence and interpretation
This establishes a much earlier Chilean network-service history than a Web-only chronology would show: domestic UUCP (1985), international UUCP email/Usenet (1986), BITNET email (1987), campus TCP/IP interconnection (1990), and direct international Internet links (January 1992). These are distinct technical and service milestones. None establishes the earliest personal homepage.
