# Samoa — early internet activity, networks, and web research

**Status:** scouting; no earliest-personal-page candidate verified.
**Focus:** locate personal or community pages enabled by mid-to-late 1990s connectivity; distinguish local infrastructure from later .ws domain marketing.

## Early connectivity, domain, and ISP context

### Domain delegation
The country-code top-level domain `.ws` (Western Samoa) was delegated by IANA on 14 July 1995.

- Source: IANA root-zone records and Wikipedia/ICANNWiki summaries of the delegation (record created 14 July 1995; sponsoring organization Government of Samoa / Ministry of Foreign Affairs).
- Evidence grade: **A** for the DNS-delegation date only. It does not prove local connectivity, a live website, or user activity.

### 1996 precursor attempt
A 1998 SOPAC field report (Leslie Allinson) states that Pacific Internet set up an ISP service in 1996 under the domain name TALOFA, linked to a commercial rugby/gambling scheme; the service was later abandoned. Equipment was later leased to Computer Services Ltd (CSL).

- Source: Martin F. / Allinson, “Internet gateway, Telecom Samoa” (SOPAC, 1998), pp. 1–2 and related summary (digital library copy).
- Evidence grade: **B/C** (contemporaneous 1998 report describing prior 1996 activity). Label as precursor / abandoned trial; not a sustained public internet service.

### 1998 shared international gateway and dial-up providers
During 7–14 March 1998, Telecom Samoa installed a shared internet gateway allowing multiple retail ISPs to reach the international satellite link. The gateway became operational around 12 March 1998 after a 12-hour switchover. Samoa.net settings (DNS, mail, web server, dial-in 25300) are recorded. Providers mentioned include Samoa.net / CSL and Lesa.

- Sources:
  - Martin F. / Allinson, SOPAC trip report “Internet gateway, Telecom Samoa” (1998).
  - Secondary summaries (btw.media and related) of the same report.
  - Later 2005 National Communications Sector Policy listing Computer Services Ltd as ISP established 1998 and Lesa with subscribers.
- Evidence grade: **B** for the March 1998 gateway installation and operational Samoa.net configuration (primary field report). Grade **C** for retrospective policy lists of founding years.

No earlier sustained public TCP/IP or dial-up service is documented in the sources reviewed. No personal-homepage candidates or community sites from 1995–1998 have been located.

## Research interpretation

- `.ws` delegation in July 1995 creates an early domain milestone, but local public access appears to open with the 1996 abandoned trial and the 1998 multi-ISP gateway.
- Samoa.net (www.samoa.net) is documented as a live host by March 1998.
- Later global marketing of `.ws` as “website” (from ~1999–2000 by Global Domains International) is external to the local early-web story and must not be treated as evidence of Samoan personal pages.
- Personal-expression and community leads remain null in current sources.
