# Early Smol Web

A collaborative research compendium of the earliest documented internet-based activity, publishing, communication, and online communities associated with every country and separately tracked territory. This includes the early Web and non-Web services such as email, mailing lists, Usenet, FTP, Gopher, IRC, connected BBS/FidoNet systems, and research or academic networks.

**Start here:** [WORK_METHOD.md](WORK_METHOD.md) · [coop.log.md](coop.log.md) · [COUNTRIES.md](COUNTRIES.md)

We run two tracks in parallel:
1. **Leads:** discover people, pages, archives, directories, communities, and historical sources.
2. **Evidence & records:** verify candidates, preserve evidence, and write consistent country entries.

The project is exploratory. "Earliest" means the earliest well-supported example of the relevant activity or medium that we can currently document, not a claim that no earlier one existed. Personal expression remains in scope, but is no longer a requirement.
