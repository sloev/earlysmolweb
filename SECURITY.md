# Security and responsible disclosure

This repository is a historical research compendium and does not intentionally collect credentials, private contact information, or sensitive personal data.

Please do not publish secrets, access tokens, passwords, private email addresses, or personal information that was not intentionally made public. If you discover such material in the repository, contact the repository owner privately rather than opening an issue that republishes it.

The research website is static. It does not require visitor accounts, cookies, analytics, or server-side submission of personal data.
